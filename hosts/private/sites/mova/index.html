Beginner calisthenics · upper body

Five Move Foundation

The five exercises and set counts from Jacob Juul Hansen's video If I Started Calisthenics Again, I'd Just Do These 5 Exercises, shown as 3D renders so you can see each position at a glance. Do them in this order, one exercise at a time.

How a session runs

  • Order1 → 5, finish all sets of one move before the next.
  • Sets3–4 sets each for moves 1–4, 2–3 sets for move 5.
  • RepsStop each set with 1–2 good reps still in you. Form first.
  • RestAbout 2 minutes between sets, a little longer for pull-ups and dips.
  • How often2–3 days a week with a rest day between.
Orange loop = resistance band taking some of your weight

The exercises and set counts are from the video. Reps, rest and weekly frequency are standard beginner guidance I've added, since they weren't on his summary.

What about legs?

The five moves cover pulling, pushing and core. He addresses legs separately in the video and points to his own leg day workout if you want to add a lower-body day.
